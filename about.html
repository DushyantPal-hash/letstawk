How it works

From hello to resolution in 3 steps.

Our voice agents are designed to understand, act, and hand off seamlessly.

1

Answer & Understand

The agent picks up immediately, listens to the caller, and uses NLU to understand intent and context.

2

Act & Resolve

Books appointments, updates records, answers FAQs, or routes to the right team member.

3

Handoff & Follow-up

If needed, seamlessly transfers to a human with full context, or sends a follow-up summary.
