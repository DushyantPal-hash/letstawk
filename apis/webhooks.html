AVAILABLE EVENTS

Subscribe to the Events That Matter to You

Choose from a comprehensive set of events to power your workflows.

call.initiated

Triggered when a call is initiated - both inbound and outbound.

call.in_progress

Sent during the call to provide real-time status updates.

call.completed

Fired when a call ends successfully with full transcript and data.

call.failed

Triggered when a call fails due to technical issues or no answer.

qualification.completed

Sent when qualification is complete with score, intent, and answers.

transcript.generated

Fired when call transcript is ready with timestamp and full text.

lead.qualified

Triggered when a lead meets qualification criteria and is ready for routing.

lead.disqualified

Sent when a lead doesn't meet qualification criteria.

lead.routed

Fired when a lead is routed to a specific team member or queue.

meeting.scheduled

Triggered when a meeting or appointment is scheduled through the AI.

follow_up.triggered

Sent when a follow-up action is triggered based on conversation outcomes.

webhook.failed

Notification when a webhook delivery fails after all retry attempts.
