THE CHALLENGE

Order Confirmation Is Being Missed or Delayed

In ecommerce and services, the moment after a purchase is often the most fragile. If customers don't get an instant confirmation, answers to basic questions, or follow-up information, they become uncertain, contact support, or abandon trust.

How It Works

From Purchase to Confirmation - Fully Automated

Letstawk keeps customers informed, reassured, and ready for the next step without manual back-and-forth.

Purchase Received

The customer completes the order or service booking and the AI recognises the transaction immediately.

Confirm Details

Letstawk verifies the order, amount, delivery window, service date, and other key details in plain language.

Explain Next Steps

It clearly tells the customer what happens next, relevant updates, and how to reach support if required.

Check for Exceptions

Payment issues, address mismatches, or service exceptions are flagged and escalated automatically.

Trigger Follow-Ups

Reminders, delivery updates, or scheduling nudges are sent based on the status of the order.

Human Handoff

Any complex or high-risk case is transferred with all details, so the team can resolve faster.
