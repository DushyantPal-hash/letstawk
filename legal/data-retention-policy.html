Data Retention Policy

How long different categories of information are kept and how deletion requests are handled.

Last updated: September 17, 2026
On this page Principles Schedule Deletion Exceptions

1. Retention principles

We retain information only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, service delivery, security, legal obligations, accounting, and dispute resolution. Retention may vary by product, customer configuration, law, and contract. Customers control retention settings for customer content where the service supports them.

2. Indicative schedule

Data Default approach
Account and contract records Term plus the period needed for tax, audit, and legal claims.
Support communications While needed to resolve the issue and improve support, then deleted or anonymized.
Call recordings and transcripts Customer-configured period; customers should set the shortest period needed.
Security and access logs Limited period needed for detection, investigation, and compliance.
Backups Deleted through ordinary backup rotation after active deletion.

Specific contractual retention periods override this indicative schedule.

3. Deletion requests

Customers may request deletion through their administrator or hello@letstawk.ai. We verify authority, delete or de-identify eligible data, and confirm completion or explain a lawful exception. Individuals should direct requests to the organization that collected their data when Letstawk is acting as processor.

4. Legal holds and exceptions

We may retain limited information for legal claims, fraud prevention, security investigations, regulatory obligations, backups, or a valid legal hold. Retained information remains protected and is deleted when the reason for retention ends.
