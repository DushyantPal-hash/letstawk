Acceptable Use Policy

Use Letstawk responsibly, lawfully, and with respect for the people reached by your voice agents.

Effective date: September 17, 2026
On this page Allowed use Prohibited conduct Enforcement Report abuse

1. Allowed use

You may use the services for legitimate business communications, customer support, scheduling, notifications, surveys, and other lawful purposes described in your order. You must have rights to the content and authority to contact recipients.

2. Prohibited conduct

  • Fraud, phishing, impersonation, scams, extortion, harassment, threats, or illegal telemarketing.
  • Malware, credential theft, unauthorized access, probing, denial of service, or attempts to bypass safeguards.
  • Unlawful discrimination, exploitation, sexual abuse, child harm, or targeting vulnerable people.
  • Unlawful collection or disclosure of personal, biometric, health, financial, or confidential information.
  • Automated decisions in high-impact domains without meaningful human review, or using outputs as professional advice.
  • Spam, caller-ID manipulation, evasion of opt-outs, or traffic designed to evade provider limits.
  • Infringing intellectual property, violating privacy or publicity rights, or breaking telecommunications or recording laws.

3. Enforcement

We may investigate, limit, suspend, or terminate access when we reasonably believe this policy is violated or necessary to protect users, the public, or the service. Where appropriate, we may request remediation, preserve evidence, notify affected parties, or refer conduct to authorities. We aim to act proportionately and may provide an appeal route.

4. Report abuse

Report suspected abuse to hello@letstawk.ai with the account or caller identifier, dates, examples, and a safe reply address. Do not include unnecessary personal information.
