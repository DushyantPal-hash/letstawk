Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear terms for subscription cancellation, usage charges, refunds, and service credits.

Effective date: September 17, 2026
On this page Cancellation Refunds Credits Request help

Your signed order form, subscription plan, or applicable consumer law may provide different rights. Those rights are not limited by this policy.

1. Cancellation and renewal

To cancel, use the account controls where available or contact us before the next renewal date. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid term; prepaid fees and committed usage are not refundable unless the order or law says otherwise. Customers remain responsible for charges incurred before cancellation.

2. Refunds

Except where required by law or stated in an order, fees are non-refundable. If Letstawk materially fails to provide a paid service and does not cure within a reasonable period, the customer may request a prorated refund for the affected unused period. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, less permitted taxes or charges.

3. Service credits and disputes

Service credits are available only where the applicable SLA or order provides them and are the customer’s exclusive remedy for the relevant availability failure. Billing disputes must be raised promptly with invoice details; undisputed charges remain payable.

4. Contact

Send cancellation or billing questions to hello@letstawk.ai. Include your account email and invoice number, but do not send payment card numbers.
