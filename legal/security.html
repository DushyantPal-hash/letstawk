Security & Compliance

Our approach to protecting customer data, maintaining resilient services, and supporting responsible use of conversational AI.

Last updated: September 17, 2026
On this page Program Access Encryption Resilience Incidents Report

1. Security program

We maintain policies and controls covering governance, risk management, secure development, change management, supplier oversight, privacy, business continuity, and incident response. Controls are reviewed periodically and adapted to the services and risks we operate.

2. Identity and access

Access is granted based on role and business need, protected with authentication controls, and reviewed periodically. Administrative actions are logged where appropriate. Workforce access to customer content is limited, monitored, and subject to confidentiality obligations.

3. Encryption and infrastructure

Data is protected in transit using modern transport encryption and at rest using platform and service encryption where supported. We use managed infrastructure, network controls, secrets management, logging, monitoring, and vulnerability remediation appropriate to the environment.

4. Availability and continuity

We use backups, redundancy, monitoring, and recovery procedures designed to reduce disruption. The specific uptime commitment and service credits, if any, are in the applicable Service Level Agreement. No system can guarantee uninterrupted availability.

5. Incident response

We maintain processes to detect, triage, contain, investigate, remediate, and learn from security incidents. Customers are notified as required by the DPA, agreement, and applicable law. We preserve relevant records and coordinate communications through authorized contacts.

6. Responsible customer configuration

Customers must secure credentials, limit user permissions, configure integrations safely, validate recipient consent, and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive data. Security controls cannot prevent harm caused by unlawful instructions or compromised customer systems.

7. Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to hello@letstawk.ai. Include affected URL or component, reproduction steps, impact, and a safe contact. Do not access or modify another person’s data, degrade service, or publicly disclose before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.
