1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone, or other device) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

Similar technologies include:

  • Pixels (web beacons): Tiny transparent images embedded in web pages or emails that track whether a page or email has been viewed.
  • Local storage: Technology that allows websites to store data locally on your device, similar to cookies but with different storage limits and expiration.
  • Session storage: Similar to local storage but data is cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Tags and scripts: Code embedded in a website that allows third parties to collect information about your interactions.
  • Fingerprinting: Techniques that identify your device based on its characteristics, such as browser version, operating system, and screen resolution.

Throughout this policy, we use the term "cookies" to refer to all these technologies collectively, unless we specifically refer to a particular type.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for several purposes, including:

  • Operating our website: To ensure our website functions properly, including navigation, security, and load balancing.
  • Authentication: To recognize you when you return to our website and keep you logged in to your account.
  • Remembering preferences: To remember your settings, such as language, region, and display preferences.
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors use our website, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve the user experience.
  • Marketing: To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns and, where permitted, to show you relevant advertisements.
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.

We only use non-essential cookies where we have obtained your consent, in accordance with applicable law. In some jurisdictions, we may rely on legitimate interests for certain analytics cookies, but you always have the right to object.

4. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for our website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, authentication, and accessibility. Without these cookies, the website cannot operate correctly.

Examples of strictly necessary cookies:

  • Session management: Cookies that maintain your session as you navigate the website.
  • Authentication: Cookies that verify your identity when you log in.
  • Security: Cookies that protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) and other security threats.
  • Load balancing: Cookies that distribute traffic across servers to ensure reliable performance.
  • Consent preferences: Cookies that remember your cookie consent choices.

Because these cookies are essential, they cannot be disabled through our cookie tool. However, you can block them through your browser settings, though this may prevent the website from functioning properly.

5. Functional cookies

Functional cookies allow our website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalized features. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Examples of functional cookies:

  • Language preferences: Remembering your preferred language.
  • Region settings: Remembering your geographic region for localized content.
  • Display preferences: Remembering settings such as dark mode or font size.
  • Form data: Remembering information you have entered in forms.
  • Embedded content: Enabling third-party content such as videos or maps.

If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of these features may not function properly. For example, you may not be able to watch embedded videos or use certain interactive features.

6. Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously or pseudonymously. This helps us improve the way our website works.

Information collected by analytics cookies may include:

  • the number of visitors to our website;
  • how visitors arrived at our website (e.g., from a search engine or referral link);
  • the pages visitors viewed and how long they stayed;
  • the actions visitors took on our website;
  • technical information such as browser type and screen resolution.

We use this information to:

  • analyze trends and usage patterns;
  • diagnose technical issues;
  • improve website performance and user experience;
  • measure the effectiveness of our content and marketing.

The analytics providers we use may combine this information with data collected from other websites to create aggregate reports. We do not use analytics cookies to identify you personally.

7. Marketing cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to:

9. Cookie duration

Cookies can be categorized by how long they remain on your device:

  • Session cookies: These are temporary cookies that remain on your device only for the duration of your browser session. They are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: These remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them manually. They are activated each time you visit the website that created them.

The specific duration of each cookie depends on its purpose and provider. For example, strictly necessary authentication cookies may last for a single session, while analytics cookies may last for up to 24 months.

You can delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Deleting cookies may affect your experience on our website, including logging you out of your account.

10. Your choices

You can change consent through our cookie banner or browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect site functionality. Browser settings do not always stop all similar technologies, and opt-out choices may be device or browser specific.

Cookie consent tool: When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie banner that allows you to accept or reject different categories of cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the footer of our website.

Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • view what cookies are stored on your device;
  • delete individual cookies or all cookies;
  • block cookies from specific websites or all websites;
  • set your browser to notify you when a cookie is set.

For more information about how to manage cookies in your browser, visit the help pages of your browser provider.
