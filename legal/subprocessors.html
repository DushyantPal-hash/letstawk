1. Current subprocessors

The providers below are representative categories used to deliver the service. The applicable customer account may use only a subset depending on configuration, region, and product.

Category Purpose Location
Cloud hosting and storage Compute, databases, backups, and content storage. Configured regional facilities
Telephony and communications Phone numbers, call routing, voice transport, and messaging. Service-dependent
AI and speech providers Speech recognition, text-to-speech, language processing, and agent operation. Service-dependent
Support and monitoring Customer support, error monitoring, security, and service analytics. Service-dependent
Payments and billing Payment collection, invoicing, and tax administration. Service-dependent

For the provider names, processing locations, and transfer mechanisms applicable to your account, contact us or consult your order documentation. We do not authorize providers to use customer content for unrelated advertising.

2. Changes

We may add or replace subprocessors to maintain or improve the service. We will provide notice where required by the agreement or law. Customers may raise a reasonable, documented objection on data protection grounds; the parties will work in good faith on a commercially reasonable solution.

3. Safeguards

We conduct risk-based diligence, contractually limit processing, require confidentiality and security controls, and remain responsible for subprocessors under the DPA.

4. Contact

Questions or objections may be sent to hello@letstawk.ai.
