Service Level Agreement

Availability, support response, maintenance, and service credit framework for eligible paid plans.

Version: September 17, 2026
On this page Scope Availability Support Credits Exclusions

This SLA applies only if incorporated into your order or plan. Trial, beta, free, and expressly excluded services do not carry an uptime commitment.

1. Scope and definitions

“Monthly uptime percentage” is the total minutes in a calendar month minus qualifying downtime, divided by total minutes, expressed as a percentage. Availability is measured at the service endpoint identified in the order, not at third-party phone networks, customer systems, or recipient devices.

2. Availability

For eligible services, Letstawk targets 99.9% monthly uptime, excluding the events in Section 5. We monitor service health and may publish status information. Planned maintenance will be announced where practicable and scheduled to reduce disruption.

3. Support

Support channels, hours, severity definitions, and target response times are stated in the order or plan. Response time is not resolution time. Customers must provide diagnostic information, maintain a supported configuration, and remain available for reasonable troubleshooting.

4. Service credits

To request a credit, contact hello@letstawk.ai within 30 days after the month of the event with account, dates, and evidence. Credits are calculated against the affected monthly recurring fee, capped at that fee, and applied to a future invoice. Credits are the sole remedy for an availability failure unless the order states otherwise.

5. Exclusions

Downtime does not include maintenance, force majeure, internet or telecommunications failures outside our control, customer or third-party systems, misuse, unsupported configurations, suspension under an agreement or policy, beta features, or failures caused by customer instructions or integrations.
