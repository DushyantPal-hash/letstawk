AI Voice & Call Recording Policy

Rules for transparent, lawful, and respectful use of Letstawk voice agents, call recording, transcription, and synthetic voices.

Effective date: September 17, 2026
On this page Disclosure Consent Controls Sensitive uses Retention

1. Clear AI disclosure

Customers must clearly tell people when they are interacting with an AI voice agent, at the beginning of the interaction or as soon as reasonably practicable. Do not represent an AI agent as a human, impersonate a person or organization, or conceal automation where disclosure is required.

2. Recording and transcription consent

Before recording or transcribing, customers must provide legally required notice and obtain consent where required. Disclosures should identify the recording purpose, retention or sharing, and available choices. Customers must comply with stricter all-party consent, telecommunications, biometric, employment, healthcare, financial, and sector-specific rules that apply to their calls.

3. Caller controls and outbound conduct

Provide an easy route to reach a human, stop automated contact, or exercise applicable rights. Honor do-not-call, do-not-contact, quiet-hour, consent withdrawal, and suppression requests promptly. Do not use caller ID spoofing, high-pressure tactics, deceptive urgency, or repeated unwanted contact. Emergency calls must not rely on the service.

4. High-impact and sensitive uses

Do not use the service as the sole basis for decisions about credit, employment, housing, insurance, education, healthcare, legal rights, or access to essential services. Add qualified human review, appropriate notices, accessible alternatives, and documented risk controls. Do not collect sensitive information unless necessary, lawful, and protected.

5. Data, quality, and retention

Configure the minimum recording and transcript retention needed for the purpose, restrict access, and securely delete data when no longer needed. Verify outputs and provide correction or escalation paths; AI can misunderstand accents, context, emotion, or intent. See the Data Retention Policy and AI Transparency page.
