AI Transparency & Disclosure

What Letstawk’s conversational AI can do, where it can fail, and how customers should keep people informed and in control.

Last updated: September 17, 2026
On this page The system Limitations Human control Data use Questions

1. What the system does

Letstawk combines speech recognition, language models, business rules, integrations, and speech synthesis to conduct configured conversations. Depending on the product, it may answer questions, qualify leads, schedule appointments, collect information, route calls, and create summaries or actions.

2. Important limitations

AI may produce incorrect, incomplete, biased, outdated, or unexpected responses; misunderstand accents, intent, sarcasm, names, or context; and fail during noise, interruptions, outages, or unusual requests. Outputs are not professional advice and must not be treated as a guarantee. Customers must test workflows and provide a human escalation route.

3. Notice and human control

Customers must disclose AI interaction and call recording as required by law, provide accessible alternatives, honor opt-outs, and ensure meaningful human review for high-impact decisions. Do not use an AI agent to impersonate a person, conceal its nature, or make a final decision about a person’s rights or access to essential services without appropriate safeguards.

4. Data and evaluation

Customer inputs and outputs are handled under the agreement and Privacy Policy. Customers should minimize sensitive data, configure retention, restrict access, and validate integrations. We monitor reliability and safety, investigate reports, and may update models or safeguards to improve performance.

5. Questions and concerns

For AI safety, accessibility, or disclosure questions, contact hello@letstawk.ai. For recording requirements, see our AI Voice & Call Recording Policy.
