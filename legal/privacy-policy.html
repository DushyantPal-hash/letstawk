1. Scope and controller

“Letstawk”, “we”, “us”, or “our” means the Letstawk entity that provides the relevant service. We act as a controller for website, marketing, account, billing, and support information. For customer content processed on a customer’s instructions, we generally act as a processor or service provider; the customer remains responsible for its notices and lawful instructions.

This policy applies to all personal data we process when you:

  • visit or interact with our website, including any subdomains or landing pages;
  • create an account, use our platform, or access our APIs;
  • communicate with our sales, support, or marketing teams;
  • attend an event, webinar, or demo that we host or co-host;
  • receive communications from us, including emails, calls, or messages.

Where we act as a processor, we process personal data only on documented instructions from our customer, unless required to do otherwise by law. In such cases, the customer’s privacy notice governs the collection and use of that data, and we encourage you to review it. Our obligations as a processor are typically set out in a data processing agreement (DPA) with the customer.

Controller vs. processor: A controller decides why and how personal data is processed. A processor handles data on behalf of a controller. For most account and billing data, Letstawk is the controller. For content you upload or process through our service on behalf of your own customers, Letstawk is typically a processor.

3. Information we collect

We collect information in three main ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third parties. The categories below describe the types of personal data we may process.

3.1 Information you provide to us

  • Contact and account data: name, work email, phone number, company, role, login credentials, and preferences.
  • Service data: prompts, configurations, call metadata, transcripts, recordings, contacts, and other content submitted by a customer or caller. This may include personal data about your end users or customers if you choose to submit it.

  • Communications: messages, demo requests, feedback, and call or support recordings where permitted by law.
  • Payment information: billing address, payment card details (processed by our payment processors), and transaction history. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: IP address, browser type and version, device identifiers, pages viewed, event logs, referring URLs, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: we use cookies, pixels, and local storage to remember preferences, authenticate sessions, measure the effectiveness of our marketing, and analyze traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings; however, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our services.
  • Log data: our servers automatically record information when you interact with our services, including the date and time of your request, your IP address, and system activity.

3.3 Information from third parties

  • Business partners and resellers: if you are referred to us or purchase through a partner, we may receive your contact and account information from that partner.
  • Public sources: we may supplement your information with data from publicly available sources, such as professional networking sites, to keep our records accurate and relevant.
  • Service providers: our analytics, advertising, and security partners may provide us with information about your interactions with our website and services.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of personal data (such as health, biometric, or precise geolocation data) unless required to provide a specific service and with your consent or as permitted by law.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where required by law (such as under the GDPR), we rely on specific legal bases to process your personal data. These are:

  • Performance of a contract: when processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you, such as providing our services or processing payments.
  • Legitimate interests: when processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, such as improving our services, preventing fraud, ensuring security, or marketing our products, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Consent: when you have given us clear consent to process your personal data for a specific purpose, such as sending marketing communications or placing certain cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: when processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, such as tax reporting or responding to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Vital interests: when processing is necessary to protect your vital interests or those of another person, such as in an emergency.

When we rely on legitimate interests, we conduct a balancing test to ensure that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not unduly impacted. You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, as described in the "Your privacy rights" section below.

7. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website and services. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us:

  • remember your preferences and settings;
  • authenticate your session and keep you logged in;
  • understand how you use our website and services;
  • measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns;
  • detect and prevent security threats and fraud.

We use the following categories of cookies:

Category Purpose Can you opt out?
Strictly necessary Required for the website to function, such as authentication and security. No — these are essential.
Functional Remember your preferences, language, and region. Yes, but disabling may affect functionality.
Analytics Help us understand how visitors use our website. Yes, through browser settings or our cookie tool.
Marketing Used to deliver relevant ads and measure campaign performance. Yes, through browser settings or our cookie tool.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. However, if you disable cookies, some parts of our services may not function properly. For more information about cookies and how to manage them, visit allaboutcookies.org.

We also honor "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals where required by law. Currently, there is no industry consensus on how to interpret DNT signals, but we will continue to monitor developments and update our practices accordingly.

9. International transfers

Letstawk operates globally, and your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. When we transfer personal data out of the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, we use appropriate safeguards to ensure your data receives an adequate level of protection.

These safeguards may include:

  • Adequacy decisions: transfers to countries that the European Commission or other relevant authority has determined provide an adequate level of data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): model contract clauses approved by the European Commission or other supervisory authorities that impose data protection obligations on the recipient.
  • Binding Corporate Rules: internal rules adopted by multinational organizations to govern international transfers within the group.
  • Other lawful mechanisms: such as derogations for specific situations (e.g., explicit consent, contract performance).

You can request more information about the safeguards we use for international transfers by contacting us at hello@letstawk.ai.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to know/access: you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.
  • Right to correction: you can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to deletion: you can ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances, such as when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Right to portability: you can request that we provide your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and transmit it to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Right to restrict processing: you can ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as while we verify the accuracy of your data.
  • Right to object: you can object to our processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests, or to direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority if you believe we have violated your privacy rights.
Note for EU/EEA residents: Under the GDPR, you have additional rights, including the right to data portability and the right to object to automated decision-making. You can find more information on the website of your national data protection authority.

18. California privacy notice

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). This section describes those rights and how to exercise them.

Categories of personal information we collect:

  • Internet or other electronic network activity (browsing history, usage data)
  • Geolocation data (approximate location derived from IP)
  • Professional or employment-related information (company, role)
  • Inferences drawn from the above to create a profile or preference

Your California rights:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, and share;
  • Right to delete personal information we have collected from you;
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information;
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information;
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise your California rights, please contact us at hello@letstawk.ai or call our toll-free number if provided. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell personal information for monetary value. However, we may share information with third-party advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising, which may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under California law. You can opt out of this sharing by contacting us or using the opt-out link on our website.
