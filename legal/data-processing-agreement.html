Data Processing Agreement

Processing terms for customer personal data handled by Letstawk on a customer’s instructions.

Version: September 17, 2026
On this page Roles and instructions Security Subprocessors Data subject rights Incidents Deletion

This DPA applies where Letstawk processes personal data for a customer as processor, service provider, or equivalent role. It supplements the order form and terms; the order form controls in a conflict.

1. Roles and documented instructions

The customer is the controller or business and Letstawk is the processor or service provider for customer data. Letstawk processes data only to provide, secure, support, and improve the contracted service as documented by the customer. The customer is responsible for lawful collection, notices, consent, data minimization, and responding to individuals.

2. Security and confidentiality

Letstawk maintains a written security program appropriate to the risk, including access controls, encryption in transit and at rest where supported, logging, vulnerability management, backups, resilience, and workforce confidentiality. Personnel with access are subject to confidentiality obligations and receive relevant training.

3. Subprocessors and transfers

Customer authorizes the subprocessors listed in our Subprocessors Policy. We remain responsible for their contracted processing, require written data protection obligations, and provide notice of material changes where required. International transfers use a lawful transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

4. Assistance with rights and compliance

Taking into account the nature of processing, we will reasonably assist with access, deletion, correction, portability, restriction, objection, impact assessments, and regulator consultations. Requests should be directed through the customer unless law requires direct response.

5. Security incidents

We will notify the customer without undue delay after confirming a breach of customer personal data and provide available details about the nature, scope, effects, and mitigation. The customer is responsible for regulatory and individual notices unless otherwise agreed.

6. Return and deletion

At the customer’s choice or on termination, we will return or delete customer data, subject to legal retention, security backups, dispute preservation, and technical limitations. Backups are isolated and deleted on their ordinary lifecycle. Confidentiality obligations survive termination.
